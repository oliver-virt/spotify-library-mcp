Da Capo — Support

Da Capo organizes your Apple Music library on your iPhone: it sorts songs into playlists, finds duplicates, resurfaces forgotten favorites, and shows the play counts Apple hides — all on-device.

Why can't it delete my duplicate songs?

Apple doesn't allow any third-party app to delete songs from your library. Da Capo does the next best thing: it gathers every duplicate into one review playlist so you can delete them in the Music app in a minute.

Do I need an Apple Music subscription?

Yes — Da Capo organizes an Apple Music library, so you need one for there to be anything to organize.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the unlock screen and tap "Restore purchase" while signed into the App Store account that bought it.
