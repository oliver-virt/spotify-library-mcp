Da Capo — Privacy Policy

Effective: August 24, 2026

Da Capo is made by Oliver Virt. Contact: orvirt@gmail.com.

The short version

Da Capo collects no data. There is no server, no account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs. The app makes no network requests.

Your music library

With your permission, Da Capo reads your Apple Music library (via Apple's MediaPlayer and MusicKit frameworks) solely to analyze it on your device and to create playlists you approve. This information never leaves your iPhone. Da Capo cannot delete your songs and cannot modify playlists it did not create — Apple's APIs do not allow it.

On-device intelligence

The optional mood-playlist feature uses Apple Intelligence, running entirely on your device. Nothing is sent to any server.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. We receive no payment details and no personal information from your purchase.

Data on your device

Scan results and preferences are stored locally on your device only, and are removed when you delete the app.

Children

Da Capo collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
