2. Soft launch NZ/Canada, 4–8 weeks, measure scan-completion, share-rate, free→paid.
3. Optional US pre-order (App Store Connect reports natively; indie cases: 650–20k preorders).
4. No fake-door tests (clicks ≠ payments).
