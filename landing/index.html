Questions

Fair questions, straight answers.

Can Da Capo delete my duplicates for me?

No app can — Apple doesn't allow third-party apps to delete songs from your library, full stop. Da Capo does the next best thing: it finds every duplicate and puts them in one review playlist, so deleting them in Music takes a minute instead of an afternoon.

Will it mess with my existing playlists?

It can't. Apple's rules only let apps edit playlists they created themselves. Da Capo's playlists are clearly named; everything else on your account is out of its reach.

Does my listening data go anywhere?

No. The scan, the stats and the sorting all happen on your iPhone. There's no server, no account and no analytics on your music. The only thing that ever leaves your phone is a report card image — if you choose to share it.

Do I need an Apple Music subscription?

Yes — Da Capo organises an Apple Music library, so you need one for there to be anything to organise.

What about Spotify?

We built the Spotify version first — then Spotify closed its API to new apps. The code is open source; if Spotify ever reopens the door, Da Capo will walk through it.
